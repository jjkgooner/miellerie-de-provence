Our story

A forgotten love story, written in honey.

Every bottle of Miellerie de Provence begins in the south of France, among lavender fields and humming beehives. Miellerie means honey house, and honey is where every scent starts.

We blend real Provence honey with dark florals, smoky woods and candlelit warmth to create perfumes that feel like a forgotten love story: sweet, but never innocent.

Hand-poured. Wax-sealed. Made to be remembered.

  • Real Provence honey Every scent contains honey from our own hives.
  • Hand-poured & wax-sealed Each bottle is filled and sealed by hand.
  • Long-lasting Eau de parfum with up to 10 hours of wear.
  • Gift-ready Wrapped in velvet, with an optional handwritten note.

The collection

Five scents. Which one haunts you?

  1. I

    Rose Noire

    The dark rose

    A rose that only blooms after dark. Velvety black rose opens onto smoky oud and settles into a warm trail of smoked honey.

    black rose · oud · smoked honey

  2. II

    Velours Rouge

    The velvet seduction

    Soft as velvet, deep as wine. Juicy black cherry melts into glowing amber and creamy vanilla.

    black cherry · amber · vanilla

  3. III

    Miel de Minuit

    The midnight honey

    Golden honey for the darkest hours. Lavender honey from our own hives, warm beeswax and a curl of incense.

    lavender honey · beeswax · incense

  4. IV

    Lavande Éternelle

    The eternal lavender

    Provence, preserved forever. Sun-warmed lavender, powdery iris and soft white musk, like a pressed flower in an old love letter.

    lavender · iris · white musk

  5. V

    Bougie Sacrée

    The sacred candle

    The warmth of a hundred candles. Resinous myrrh, melted wax and dried petals for slow evenings.

    myrrh · candle wax · dried petals

Questions

Before you fall in love

How long does the scent last?

Our perfumes are eau de parfum and last up to 8–10 hours on the skin, longer on clothing.

Which scent should I choose?

Love sweet and warm? Try Miel de Minuit or Velours Rouge. Prefer dark and mysterious? Rose Noire. Soft and nostalgic? Lavande Éternelle. Cosy and smoky? Bougie Sacrée. Still unsure? The Discovery Set lets you try all five.

Is the honey real?

Yes. We use honey extract from our own hives in Provence in every blend.

What happens with my data?

We only use your name and email address to send you our newsletter, and only if you give permission. You can unsubscribe at any moment via the link in every email.
